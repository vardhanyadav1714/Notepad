Notes 1.6: reliable page creation and continuous scrolling

Latest update: 1.7 restores finger writing by default and adds a visible Write/Scroll mode label. The S Pen default described below applies only to 1.6.

Tested on the connected Samsung Galaxy Tab SM-T733 on 15 October 2026. Notes Preview 1.6 (22) is installed alongside the original app. Existing notes and collaboration settings were preserved.

Reproduced failure

A two-finger swipe with 12% spacing drift failed to append a page in 1.5, while a parallel swipe succeeded. The recognizer permanently treated any span change above 3% as a pinch. Finger writing also defaulted to enabled on S Pen tablets, so a normal one-finger scroll could write instead. The end-pull preview jumped by 72dp and scrolling stopped immediately on lift.

Changes

  • Scroll-versus-pinch detection compares span motion against finger translation, with touch slop. Small spacing drift stays a scroll. A deliberate pinch, including one begun after scrolling or at a zoom limit, cancels provisional page creation.
  • The next page is revealed continuously during an end pull, with a 40dp commit threshold. Pulling back can cancel it. A short uncommitted pull returns over 180ms. One gesture adds at most one page and preserves undo.
  • Scrolling continues when one finger lifts from a two-finger swipe. That remaining finger cannot resume writing.
  • Velocity-based momentum browses existing pages after release. A new canvas contact cancels it immediately. Momentum cannot append pages.
  • S Pen devices default to finger scrolling and pen writing. Explicit finger-writing preferences are respected, with a visible tablet toolbar toggle.
