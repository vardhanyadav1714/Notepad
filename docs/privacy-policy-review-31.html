Your notes and attachments

Drawly stores notes, drawings, imported images and PDFs, audio recordings, and preferences in app storage on your device. The reviewed app does not require an account. Microphone permission is requested for recordings; you can decline it and continue using other features. Imported files are selected through Android's file picker. Note locking uses Android device authentication; it is not separate encryption of note files.

Optional shared drawing

When you create or join a collaboration room, drawing strokes and their associated identifiers, page identifiers, timing, pressure, and tool settings are sent over an encrypted connection to our Cloudflare-hosted service. Participants with the room link can receive the shared drawing. Treat the link as access to the room, and share it only with people you trust. Do not include private information in shared drawings. The reviewed collaboration feature does not synchronize typed note blocks, audio recordings, imported images or PDFs.

Room contents are stored to allow participants to reconnect. The reviewed server schedules deletion after 24 hours of inactivity, with active connections extending the room lifetime. Leaving a room or deleting a local note does not immediately erase the server copy or copies held by other participants. Owner verification required: confirm this behavior on the deployed server and document hosting/access-log retention and the procedure for responding to room-deletion requests.

Handwriting recognition

Drawly uses Google ML Kit for optional on-device handwriting recognition and downloads language models as needed. Recognition runs on the device. ML Kit documents collection of device/application information, installation identifiers, performance and diagnostic information, and configured recognition languages. See Google's ML Kit data disclosures and Google's privacy policy. Confirm the disclosures against the exact SDK version shipped.

Backups, exports and deletion

Android backup and device transfer may include app data depending on your device and account settings. Exporting or sharing content sends it to the destination you choose; that destination's practices also apply. You can move notes to Trash and delete them permanently from the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app storage; exported files, backups and other participants' copies may remain. Manage these copies through the relevant device settings or service.

Children and shared content — decision required

The owner confirmed that Play Console targets every age group, including children aged five and under, and includes Drawly for Teacher Approved review. Sharing safeguards and SDK use must match that audience. The reviewed app currently lacks the required adult management and safety reminder for its shared drawing feature.
