Notes 1.2: reference-driven reconstruction

Latest: Notes 1.3 editor features and collaboration validation.

15 October 2026 · Version 1.2 (18). This pass reconstructs the library, navigation, settings, page previews and contextual controls around the supplied Samsung Notes 4.4.13.5 APK and Samsung’s published interface references.

This is an independent implementation of the core interface and local editing tools. Full Samsung Notes parity and pixel-identical matching have not been established. No Samsung executable code, fonts, logos or extracted artwork are bundled in the app.

What changed

SurfaceResult
LibraryLarge centered heading collapses while scrolling. Compact navigation/search/options row, sort label, portrait page covers, titles and dates beneath previews, grid/list views, simple empty state and one create-note button. A long press opens a selection state with Move, Favourite and Delete at the bottom; All selects the visible notes.
DrawerSettings at the top, restrained selected row, All notes, Favourites, Trash, coloured folders and Manage folders. Standalone sketchbook stays accessible below the main navigation.
Page previewsThe renderer now produces a miniature first page with actual coordinates and margins, rather than cropping tightly around the ink. Preview cache identity changes with the note’s modification timestamp. Opening a later page does not replace the note’s cover.
EditorCompact back/read/insert/options header. Reading mode suppresses drawing controls and blocks ink input. Main options lead to Share, Save as file, Page template, Pages, finger drawing, full screen and More tools; advanced actions are grouped into submenus.
PensSix nib choices, stroke preview rendered by the actual pressure engine, quick thickness presets, slider, colour swatches and favourite toggle. Ballpoint and fountain have independent settings, as do pencil, brush, calligraphy and marker. Their configurations survive switching and restart.
Paper and settingsFour visual template choices show the actual ruling renderer; six background colours plus a custom picker. Settings uses grouped summary rows and selection dialogs for theme, view and sorting, with a working finger-drawing switch.

Still outside parity

Samsung account/cloud, Samsung .sdoc/.sdocx compatibility, AI features, image OCR, transcription, Office export, widgets, screen-off memo and Samsung-specific device gestures are not implemented. Rich text uses positioned page objects rather than Samsung’s flowing document layout. Lasso selects ink; it does not group text and images with ink. Images support movement and resizing, not crop/rotation. The magnified pad advances manually. Note locks are device-authentication gates, not encrypted files. Successful handwriting-model download, physical S Pen latency/palm behaviour, biometric hardware, microphone hardware and live collaboration require device/network validation. Text, images and audio are local; the collaboration worker has not been deployed.
